# Backdrop brief (the same four scenes every round)

Two films use these backdrops: a short film "末班车" (Clawd carries a potted sprout onto a night
train) and a promo where each of the 400 Clawd GIFs plays in a scene, then gets framed. Clawd and his
props are drawn separately on top — leave his stage, don't draw him. Every scene is one 1920 x 1080
frame, front-on, in the reference videos' halftone style.

Clawd's size: 224 px wide body, 210 px tall with legs, standing feet at y = 975 unless noted.

1. **carriage_night** (dark theme) — inside a night train carriage, seen straight on from the aisle.
   Far wall with two big windows (outside: a moonlit lake — the moon with a halo high in the left
   window, its reflection on calm water, far hills); curtains tied at the window sides; a luggage rack
   with a suitcase above; a ceiling lamp whose light pools on the table; a table under the left window;
   a bench on the left (Clawd sits on it: his body spans x 110–335, seat top at y 840) and an empty
   bench on the right; wall panelling below the windows. Light: the lamp (warm pool) and the moon (cold
   patch through the window onto the table).

2. **station_night** (dark theme) — a small city railway platform at night, side view. The side of a
   passenger car fills the upper middle (roof, a row of lit windows, a door left of centre, the
   bogies hidden by the platform edge); the platform in front with its edge stripe; two lamp posts with
   pools of light; a bench; a station clock on a post showing 23:59; a name board "城南站"; behind
   and above the train: a station canopy and a faint city skyline. Clawd stands on the platform at
   x 260–485, feet at y 975.

3. **home_day** (light theme) — a cosy living room in daylight: a big window with the sun and a far
   city; a sofa (Clawd stands in front of it, centre, x 850–1075); plants in pots of different sizes;
   a bookshelf with books and small objects; a floor lamp; a rug; a picture on the wall.

4. **park_day** (light theme) — a park: sky with two clouds and the sun; far city skyline and hills
   (faint); trees (near trees with shaded foliage and trunks); a bench; a path; grass tufts; a lamp
   post. Clawd stands on the path, centre, x 850–1075.

## Deliverables (in the iteration folder you are given)

- `scenes/<scene>.svg`, `scenes/<scene>.png` (svg2dots render), `scenes/<scene>_value.png`
- `scenes/<scene>_cmp.png` — compare.py against the closest reference frame in the skill's assets/refs
- `scenes/<scene>_crops.png` — two or three 1:1 crops of your render next to 1:1 crops of the same kind
  of object from the reference frame (e.g. a plant, a window, a piece of furniture)
- your scripts, and `notes.md`
